Aim High, Shoot Straight, Have Fun

Welcome to the official IIUM Archery Club website. Join us to discover the art of archery, a sunnah sport that strengthens both body and mind.

About Us

Our Mission

To be a leading student organization that provides a fun and relaxing environment for students to enjoy and practice archery, a sport that has many benefits for the students and the society.

Our Vision

To see a generation of IIUM students who are healthy, happy, and skilled in archery, and who are able to apply the sunnah of archery and the values and virtues of the sunnah in their personal and professional lives.

Objectives

  • To provide a platform for students to learn and practice archery.
  • To create a fun and relaxing environment for socialization.
  • To organize and participate in various archery events, tournaments, and workshops.
  • To promote and appreciate the sunnah of archery.

Club Constitution

The core governing rules of our organization.

The name of this club shall be the Archery Club, hereinafter referred to as the AC.

The objectives include promoting physical and mental health, creating a social environment, organizing events, and including a sunnah component.

Open to all IIUM students. Requires a membership application, RM 10 annual fee, and agreement to the code of conduct.

Positions include President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, and Committee Members. Elected annually.

For the full constitution, please refer to the official documents or contact the secretary.

Resources & Tips

Leadership Continuity

  • Recruit and train new leaders: Identify potential successors and provide mentorship.
  • Create and update club documents: Maintain constitution, budget, and activity plans.
  • Build and maintain relationships: Engage with stakeholders, advisors, and other clubs.

Management Tips

  • Define vision clearly.
  • Plan activities in advance.
  • Delegate tasks effectively.
  • Prioritize safety and etiquette.
