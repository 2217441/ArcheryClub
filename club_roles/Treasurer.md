# Role: Club Treasurer

## Responsibilities
*   **Financial Stewardship**: Manage the club's bank account and cash flow.
*   **Budgeting**: Prepare financial reports and budget proposals for events.
*   **Collection**: Collect membership fees (RM 10) and event participation fees.

## Runbook
*   **Monthly**:
    1.  Reconcile bank statements.
    2.  Report current financial standing to the President.
*   **End of Semester**:
    1.  Prepare the audited financial report for the AGM.
