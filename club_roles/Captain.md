# Role: Club Captain

## Responsibilities
*   **Training**: Design and lead training sessions for beginners and the elite team.
*   **Equipment**: Maintain the club's inventory (Bows, Arrows, Targets) and ensure safety standards.
*   **Selection**: Select archers to represent the university in competitions (e.g., SUKIPT, MASUM).

## Runbook
*   **Weekly Training**:
    1.  Arrive 30 mins early to set up the range.
    2.  Conduct safety briefing before every session.
*   **Maintenance**:
    1.  Inspect strings and limbs for damage once a month.
