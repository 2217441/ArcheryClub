# Role: Club Secretary

## Responsibilities
*   **Documentation**: Maintain accurate records of all meetings (Minutes), membership lists, and official correspondence.
*   **Communication**: Manage the club's official email (`archery@iium.edu.my`) and disseminate notices to members.
*   **Bureaucracy**: Handle booking of venues (Sports Complex, Meeting Rooms) through the university system.

## Runbook
*   **Post-Meeting**:
    1.  Draft minutes within 48 hours.
    2.  Share minutes with the ExCo for approval.
*   **Event Preparation**:
    1.  Prepare proposal documents for STADD approval 1 month in advance.
