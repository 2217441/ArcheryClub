# Role: Club President

## Responsibilities
*   **Strategic Leadership**: Define the club's vision for the academic year.
*   **External Representation**: Represent the club in meetings with STADD (Student Affairs) and other university bodies.
*   **Team Management**: Oversee the Executive Committee and ensure all departments are functioning.

## Runbook
*   **Start of Semester**:
    1.  Convening the first General Meeting (AGM/EGM).
    2.  Reviewing and approving the semester budget.
*   **Weekly**:
    1.  Chairing the ExCo meeting.
    2.  Signing off on official letters and proposals.
